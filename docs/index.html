The Avalanche briefing

OT point, but a loss. Steven Stamkos scored on a power play at 2:18 of overtime as Nashville beat Colorado 3–2 at Ball Arena. The Avs are now 9–6–2 (20 pts, still 4th in the Central) after taking the point. Next: Saturday vs Detroit, 7:00 PM MST on ALT. DraftKings has not posted Saturday’s board yet on ESPN. Kalshi has Colorado at 67¢ for Saturday.

Published Fri Nov 6, 2026 · ~7:35 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Thursday’s (Nov 5) edition.

Record

9–6–220 pts · 4th in Central

Seventeen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 68 goals for, 56 against (+12). The Avs are 6–1–2 at home and 3–5–0 on the road, 5–4–1 in the last 10, and coming off an OT loss (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central with 25 points in 17 games; Utah has 21 in 17. Dallas and Colorado have 20 each (Stars in 16 GP). Nashville has 19 after last night’s win, St. Louis 16, Winnipeg 15 and Chicago 9. The Avs are 6th in the West and 13th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 27 points (9 G, 18 A); Necas 19 (7 G, 12 A); Makar 18 (4 G, 14 A in 16 games); Lehkonen 17 (team-high 12 G); Toews 15 (5 G, 10 A); Kadri 14 (5 G, 9 A); Schwartz 12 (7 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 8–3–1, .893, 2.81; Wedgewood 1–3–1, .867, 4.02. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Thu Nov 5 vs Predators: OTL 2–3. Ross Colton scored against his former club at 4:01 of the first (O’Reilly). Jaden Schwartz tied it at 9:11 off Necas’s cross-slot feed (Nelson also assisted). Vitali Pinchuk then scored his first NHL goal at 14:34, a backhand over Blackwood’s shoulder (Schaefer). Parker Kelly evened it 2–2 at 5:08 of the second on a rush snap shot (O’Connor, Juulsen) — Kelly’s third of the season. A scoreless third featured big Blackwood stops, including on O’Reilly’s PP chance and Wilsby at 4-on-4. In overtime, Devon Toews hit the post at 1:42, then was called for hooking on Stamkos 20 seconds later. Stamkos one-timed Josi’s feed (O’Reilly also assisted) at 2:18 of OT for the winner — his 247th career power-play goal, tying Luc Robitaille for fifth all-time (NHL.com / AP). Justus Annunen (ex-Avalanche) made 30 saves. Colorado outshot Nashville 32–25 but went 0-for-3 on the power play while Nashville finished 1-for-5. Predators blocked 25 shots. Bednar liked the defensive effort after Tuesday’s blowout: “There’s no doubt in my mind that we played harder and more committed on the defending side… We certainly had our looks to try and win it” (NHL.com). MacKinnon played his 967th career game, tying Adam Foote for third in Avalanche/Nordiques history.
