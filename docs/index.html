Saturday, Nov 14, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day at Montreal

The Avalanche briefing

The Avs play at Montreal tonight (5:00 PM MST, ALT2 / KTVD), and the status of MacKinnon and Makar is still unconfirmed. Both are listed day to day with lower-body injuries after leaving Thursday’s 4–3 overtime loss at Ottawa. Colorado recalled forward Gavin Brindley from the Eagles on Friday (NHL.com). Colorado is 11–6–3 with 25 pts, 3rd in the Central. DraftKings has Montreal −122 / Colorado +102 (total 6.5) on ESPN’s scoreboard. Kalshi has the Avs at 47¢ (50¢ yesterday) and the Canadiens at 54¢. Cup odds shortened to +700 from +750.

Published Sat Nov 14, 2026 · ~7:45 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Friday’s (Nov 13) edition. The Avs did not play Friday, so the last game is still Thursday’s at Ottawa.

Record

11–6–325 pts · 3rd in Central

Twenty games into 2026–27 (no game Friday) (W–L–OT): 81 goals for, 63 against (+18). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 4–5–1 on the road, 6–2–2 in the last 10, and their streak is one overtime loss (NHL standings API). Minnesota leads the Central with 32 points in 21 games. Nashville is 2nd with 27 (20 GP, after Friday’s win) and Colorado is 3rd with 25 in the same 20 games. Dallas and Utah have 22, Winnipeg 21, St. Louis 20 and Chicago 11. The Avs are 5th in the West and 11th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A); Necas 24 (10 G, 14 A); Makar 23 (5 G, 18 A in 19 games); Lehkonen 19 (team-high 14 G); Toews 19 (6 G, 13 A); Kadri 16 (5 G, 11 A); Schwartz 13 (7 G, 6 A); Landeskog 13 (8 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 10–3–1, .901, 2.63, 1 shutout; Wedgewood 1–3–2, .863, 3.99. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game (Thu Nov 12)

Thu Nov 12 at Senators: L 3–4 (OT). Ottawa took a 2–0 lead in the first period: Shane Pinto scored at 7:11 and Tim Stützle at 13:13 (Zetterlund, Cozens). Colorado got on the board at 18:04 of the second when Artturi Lehkonen scored on the power play (Kadri, Hughes), his 14th. In the third, Parker Kelly tied it 2–2 shorthanded at 3:54 (unassisted) and Gabriel Landeskog gave Colorado a 3–2 lead on the power play at 10:03 (Nelson, Toews), his 8th. Drake Batherson tied it at 16:04 (Spence, Pinto), grabbing a loose puck in the crease with 3:56 left. In overtime Pinto, on his 26th birthday, intercepted a pass in the Avs’ zone and scored on a wrist shot at 2:08 for the win. Scott Wedgewood stopped 22 of 26 for Colorado; Linus Ullmark, back for the first time since Oct. 21, stopped 26 of 29 for Ottawa (AP). MacKinnon left with 8:15 left in the second after a reverse hit on Artem Zub and grabbed his right side; Makar left a few minutes earlier and returned for one 23-second shift (NHL.com). Both are listed day to day with lower-body injuries. Bednar: “You’re running without a couple of horses for half the game… pretty gutsy effort from our group… It’s a big point.”
