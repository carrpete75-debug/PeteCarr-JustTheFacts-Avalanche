Thursday, Nov 12, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day at Ottawa

The Avalanche briefing

Avs at Senators tonight, 5:00 PM MST on ALT. Colorado (11–6–2, 24 pts) has not played since Tuesday’s 6–3 win at Toronto (Landeskog 2 G; Blackwood 28 saves) and slipped to 3rd in the Central when Nashville won again and reached 25 points. DraftKings now has the Avs −120 on the moneyline (Senators +100), puck line −1.5 at +210 and a total of 6.5 (over −102 / under −118). Kalshi has the Avs at 52¢, down from 55¢. Cup odds are unchanged at +700. ESPN lists Mackenzie Blackwood and Linus Ullmark as expected starters; neither team has confirmed its goalie, and ESPN’s injury list has Ullmark day to day.

Record

11–6–224 pts · 3rd in Central

Nineteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 78 goals for, 59 against (+19). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 4–5–0 on the road, 6–3–1 in the last 10, and they’ve won two straight (NHL standings API; no game Wednesday). Minnesota leads the Central with 30 points in 20 games. Nashville is 2nd with 25 (19 GP, six straight wins) and Colorado is 3rd with 24, ahead of Dallas (22 in 17 GP) and Utah (21). Winnipeg has 19, St. Louis 18 and Chicago 9. Colorado has points in six of its last seven games (5–1–1, NHL.com). The Avs are 5th in the West and 10th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A); Necas 24 (10 G, 14 A); Makar 23 (5 G, 18 A in 18 games); Lehkonen 18 (team-high 13 G); Toews 18 (6 G, 12 A); Kadri 15 (5 G, 10 A); Schwartz 13 (7 G, 6 A); Landeskog 12 (7 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 10–3–1, .901, 2.63, 1 shutout; Wedgewood 1–3–1, .867, 4.02. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Tue Nov 10 at Maple Leafs: W 6–3. Darren Raddysh’s one-timer at 3:33 of the first (Matthews, Roslovic) gave Toronto the early lead, his first goal as a Leaf. Colorado answered late in the period: Brock Nelson tied it at 16:09 (Hughes, Schwartz) and Landeskog jammed in a rebound at 17:26 for a 2–1 lead, 77 seconds later (Roy, Makar). Toews made it 3–1 at 3:31 of the second with a point shot through traffic (O’Connor, Kelly). Easton Cowan cut it to 3–2 at 19:31 of the second (McKenna, Paul), and Jack Roslovic tied it 3–3 on a breakaway at 4:11 of the third (Cowan, Raddysh). Colorado answered 51 seconds later: Landeskog redirected Kadri’s cross-crease pass at 5:02 for his second of the game and the winner (Kadri, Malinski). Lehkonen made it 5–3 on a breakaway at 17:15 (Makar, Toews), and Necas scored into an empty net at 17:30 for the 6–3 final (his 10th). Blackwood stopped 28 of 31; Sergei Bobrovsky stopped 22 of 27 for Toronto, which has lost four in a row (AP). Thirteen different Avs recorded a point (ColoradoAvalanche.com). Makar had left Sunday’s practice early with a lower-body issue but declared himself ready after the morning skate and had two assists, five over the last two games (Denver Sports; NHL.com). Bednar: “I think it’s as close as you can get [to a 60-minute effort].”
