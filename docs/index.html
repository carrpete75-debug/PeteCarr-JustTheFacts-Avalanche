Wednesday, Oct 21, 2026 · Morning Edition

The Avalanche briefing

Back-to-back ends with two losses. The Devils beat Colorado 4–3 in Newark Tuesday. The Avs trailed 4–1 before Artturi Lehkonen scored twice in the final 3:13; the second came with seconds left (19:55). Gabriel Landeskog also scored, Cale Makar had two assists, and Scott Wedgewood made 37 saves. New Jersey outshot Colorado 41–34. “We looked slow and unenergetic,” Bednar said (NHL.com). Colorado is 5–4–1 (11 pts), has lost two straight, and has slipped to 4th in the Central. Next: Friday vs Tampa Bay at 7:00 PM MDT on ALT / The Spot, with Logan O’Connor expected to make his season debut. DraftKings lines not posted yet. No roster moves. DK Cup +700, West +350, Central +170 (all flat). Kalshi has Friday at 58¢ and the Central fell 6¢ to 36¢.

Published Wed Oct 21, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:45 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Tuesday’s (Oct 20) edition.

Record

5–4–111 pts · 4th in Central

Ten games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 41 goals for, 31 against (+10); 3–1–1 at home and 2–3–0 on the road. Colorado has lost two straight and is 1–2–1 in its last four (NHL.com). Dallas beat Boston in overtime Tuesday to pull level on 11 points with a game in hand, so the Avs drop from 3rd to 4th. They are 7th in the West and 15th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 16 points (6 G, 10 A); Lehkonen 11 (team-high 8 G); Necas 11 (5 G, 6 A); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 9 games); Nelson 8; Kadri 7 (all assists); Toews 7. In goal: Blackwood 4–3–0, .889, 2.90; Wedgewood 1–1–1, .912, 2.65. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Tue Oct 20 @ Devils: L 3–4. New Jersey controlled the first period, outshooting Colorado 15–4. T.J. Hughes blocked a Timo Meier shot to keep it scoreless early (ColoradoAvalanche.com), but Luke Evangelista, back from a three-game illness, scored from the slot at 6:40. Landeskog tied it at 14:32. Makar took Nazem Kadri’s drop pass at the point, stickhandled between Meier’s legs, skated past Jack Hughes and fed the captain alone at the right post. It was Makar’s 300th career even-strength point (team site). The Devils outshot Colorado 17–9 in the second, and Declan Chisholm’s point shot deflected off Brock Nelson’s stick and past Wedgewood at 12:33 for a 2–1 lead. Arseny Gritsyuk split Makar and Devon Toews for a backhand goal at 4:39 of the third, and Jesper Boqvist hit the empty net at 16:05. Colorado then put 21 shots on Jake Allen in the third. Lehkonen scored from the right circle off Gavin Brindley’s feed at 16:47, then scored again from the low slot with Wedgewood pulled at 19:55. Allen made 31 saves, 19 of them in the third. “We were losing races, losing battles. We waited too long to start playing,” Bednar said (NHL.com). Colorado Hockey Now called it “a second consecutive listless effort.” Brent Burns played his 1,015th consecutive game (AP).
