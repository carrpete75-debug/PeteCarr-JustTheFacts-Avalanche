Friday, Oct 30, 2026 · Morning Edition · Off day

The Avalanche briefing

Up to third without playing. Colorado was off Thursday, and Dallas, Minnesota and Nashville all lost, so the 7–5–1 Avs (15 pts) moved up to 3rd in the Central. They are level with Dallas on points and games played and ahead on regulation wins. Josh Manson (lower body) is still listed out; ESPN estimates a Nov 5 return, and the team hasn’t given a timeline or made a roster move. Next: Sat vs Calgary, 8:00 PM MDT, ALT. The Flames host Seattle tonight, so they arrive on a back-to-back. DraftKings lines aren’t posted yet. Kalshi has the Avs at 75¢ Saturday (−2¢).

Published Fri Oct 30, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:40 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Thursday’s (Oct 29) edition.

Record

7–5–115 pts · 3rd in Central

Thirteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 54 goals for, 42 against (+12); 5–1–1 at home and 2–4–0 on the road; 5–4–1 in the last 10; one straight win (NHL.com). Idle Thursday. Minnesota leads the Central on 21 and Utah has 18. Colorado and Dallas have 15 each in 13 games, and the Avs rank 3rd on regulation wins (7 to Dallas’s 4). St. Louis and Nashville have 13, Winnipeg 11 and Chicago 8. Colorado is 6th in the West and 16th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 22 points (8 G, 14 A); Necas 16 (6 G, 10 A); Lehkonen 15 (team-high 10 G); Toews 11 (4 G, 7 A); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 12 games); Kadri 10 (2 G, 8 A); Schwartz 9; Nelson 8; Landeskog 8. In goal: Blackwood 6–3–0, .894, 2.81; Wedgewood 1–2–1, .889, 3.48. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Wed Oct 28 vs Devils: W 5–2. New Jersey led on a short-handed goal. Connor Brown intercepted MacKinnon’s centering pass, broke out on a 2-on-1 with Luke Hughes, and his pass went in off Cale Makar’s skate and through Blackwood at 12:21 of the first (NHL.com). Josh Manson left at 15:19 of the first after falling awkwardly on a missed check. Landeskog tied it on the power play at 10:58 of the second, finishing Schwartz’s cross-crease pass. The Avs outshot the Devils 21–6 in that period, but Jake Allen held. The third period broke it open. Kadri finished Nicolas Roy’s pass at 2:45, and Kelly beat Allen short side at 3:47. MacKinnon split Hughes and Brett Pesce for a breakaway goal at 12:00, and Schwartz scored on Malinski’s rebound 30 seconds later. Brown added a late backhand. “There was a simplicity to our offense. It was highly effective,” Bednar said. “I think it’s repeatable, regardless of your opponent” (NHL.com). Burns’ two assists gave him 950 career points, 10th among NHL defensemen. Roy had an assist in 11:26 in his return after six games out. Bednar’s 452nd win tied Joel Quenneville for ninth-most with one franchise, and Landeskog passed Cody McLeod for the franchise hits record (NHL.com).
