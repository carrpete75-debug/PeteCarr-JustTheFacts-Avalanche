Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the Central and first in the NHL. Playoffs: reached the Western Conference Final and were swept 4–0 by Vegas (AP).

Saturday · Oct 3 vs Blues

Martin Necas scored twice and added an assist, Nathan MacKinnon had three assists, and the Avalanche routed the St. Louis Blues 6–1 at Ball Arena. Colorado has scored six or more goals in each of its first two games. Cale Makar opened the scoring at 4:09 of the first, Necas made it 2–0 later in the period, and then Josh Manson, Artturi Lehkonen, Nicolas Roy and Necas again blew it open in a four-goal second.

Mackenzie Blackwood stopped 25 of 26 shots; Jordan Binnington allowed all six Colorado goals on 36 shots. Dalibor Dvorsky spoiled the shutout at 2:34 of the third. Three stars: Necas, MacKinnon, Lehkonen. Attendance 18,087.

Next: First road trip of the season starts Wednesday at Winnipeg (5:30 PM MDT, ALT), then Thursday at Calgary.
