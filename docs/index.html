Record

4–2–08 pts · 4th in Central

Six games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 29 goals for, 19 against; 2–1–0 at home and 2–1–0 on the road; winners of one straight. Utah (5–2–0, 10 pts) leads the Central. Winnipeg (9 pts in 6 GP) and Minnesota (9 in 7) follow, then Colorado at 8 in 6. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Tue Oct 13 @ Stars: W 3–2. Artturi Lehkonen opened the scoring at 11:32 of the first, knocking in a Josh Manson point shot that had deflected off MacKinnon’s skate. Tyler Seguin tied it on the power play at 19:06 when his pass went in off Lehkonen’s skate. Necas put Colorado ahead 2–1 at 3:05 of the second, one-timing a give-and-go with MacKinnon. He scored again late in the second on a 5-on-3 (16:14 per the NHL gamecenter API; NHL.com’s recap says 16:07), banging in Landeskog’s pass off a Toews rebound. Roope Hintz pulled Dallas within one at 3:17 of the third, but Mackenzie Blackwood shut the door from there with 24 saves on 26 shots. Bednar called it “probably his best performance of the year.” MacKinnon had two assists (12 points), and he fought Hintz in the second and needed facial repairs before returning. Colorado played 17 skaters: Makar stayed in Denver with his wife, Tracy, who is expecting twins, and Zachary L’Heureux was a late scratch (upper body) after warmups. Fedor Svechkov’s instigator, fighting major and misconduct then left the Avs with 10 forwards for nearly 17 minutes (AP, NHL.com).
