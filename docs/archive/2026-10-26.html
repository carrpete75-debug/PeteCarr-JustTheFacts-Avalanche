Monday, Oct 26, 2026 · Morning Edition · Off day

The Avalanche briefing

Predators 6, Avalanche 2. Nashville scored four power-play goals, and former Av Ryan O’Reilly had two goals and two assists. Artturi Lehkonen (his 10th) and Devon Toews scored for Colorado, and Scott Wedgewood made 29 saves on 35 shots. The Avs are 6–5–1 (13 pts), still 4th in the Central, but Minnesota (18) and Dallas (15) won and Nashville is one point back. Next: Wednesday vs New Jersey, 7:00 PM MDT at Ball Arena on ALT. ESPN upgraded Nicolas Roy to day-to-day, and Georgii Merkulov cleared waivers. Wednesday’s DraftKings lines are not posted yet. DK Cup +700, West +350, Central +170 (all flat). Kalshi has the Avs at 64¢ for Wednesday.

Published Mon Oct 26, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:40 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Sunday’s (Oct 25) edition.

Record

6–5–113 pts · 4th in Central

Twelve games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 49 goals for, 40 against (+9); 4–1–1 at home and 2–4–0 on the road; one straight loss (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central on 18 points, Utah has 16 and Dallas 15; the Stars are two ahead of the Avs with a game in hand. Nashville is one point behind on 12, and St. Louis and Winnipeg are two back on 11. Colorado is 7th in the West and 16th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 21 points (7 G, 14 A); Necas 16 (6 G, 10 A); Lehkonen 14 (team-high 10 G); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 11 games); Toews 10 (4 G, 6 A); Kadri 8; Nelson 8. In goal: Blackwood 5–3–0, .893, 2.91; Wedgewood 1–2–1, .889, 3.48. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Sun Oct 25 at Predators: L 2–6. Parker Kelly went off for hooking at 2:37, and Filip Forsberg scored on that power play at 4:01 from the high slot after Steven Stamkos blocked a clearing attempt (AP). Stamkos made it 2–0 at 10:48. It was ruled no goal on the ice when Cale Makar swept the puck off the line, but video review found it had fully crossed (AP). In the second, O’Reilly scored on a power play at 6:38, Jack Drury made it 4–0 at 16:36, and O’Reilly scored on another power play at 17:29. Lehkonen answered 26 seconds later off Necas’ cross-ice feed. Toews scored 4-on-4 at 2:43 of the third, but Stamkos finished a 5-on-3 at 10:24 for the 6–2 final. Colorado took eight minors, including a too-many-men bench penalty. Kelly also fought Jayden Struble. Nashville outshot the Avs 35–21 and won 37 of 60 faceoffs. “We should have been the team with more energy … with (Nashville) playing the back-to-back,” Jared Bednar said. “It looked like it was the exact opposite.” He added that staying out of the box “was the exact opposite too” (NHL.com). Justus Annunen, traded to Nashville for Wedgewood last November, made 19 saves for his first win of the season. The AP noted Colorado has lost three of four.
