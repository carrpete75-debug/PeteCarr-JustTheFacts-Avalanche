Monday, Nov 2, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day

The Avalanche briefing

Game day in Chicago. The Avs (8–5–1, 17 pts, 3rd in the Central) visit the last-place Blackhawks (4–9–1) at 6:00 PM MST on ALT. It’s the first half of a back-to-back, with Columbus on Tuesday. DraftKings has Colorado at −238 (Chicago +195), puck line −1.5 at +110, total 6.5. Kalshi has the Avs at 68¢ (−3¢). Blackwood is the projected starter, but neither team has confirmed its goalie. Roster: Gavin Brindley was reassigned to the AHL Eagles on Sunday. Minnesota won Sunday and leads the Central by six points. Futures didn’t move.

Published Mon Nov 2, 2026 · ~7:40 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Sunday’s (Nov 1) edition.

Record

8–5–117 pts · 3rd in Central

Fourteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT), unchanged after Sunday’s off day: 59 goals for, 44 against (+15); 6–1–1 at home and 2–4–0 on the road; 5–4–1 in the last 10; two straight wins (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central on 23 after winning 5–1 at Washington on Sunday; Utah has 18. Colorado and Dallas have 17 each in 14 games, and the Avs lead on regulation wins (8 to 5). St. Louis and Nashville have 15, Winnipeg 11 and Chicago 9. Colorado is 5th in the West and 13th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 24 points (9 G, 15 A); Necas 18 (7 G, 11 A); Lehkonen 17 (team-high 12 G); Makar 15 (3 G, 12 A in 13 games); Toews 13 (4 G, 9 A); Kadri 11 (2 G, 9 A); Nelson, Schwartz and Landeskog 9 each. In goal: Blackwood 7–3–0, .897, 2.73; Wedgewood 1–2–1, .889, 3.48. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Sat Oct 31 vs Flames: W 5–2. Calgary was on the second night of a back-to-back and started its No. 3 goalie, Arsenii Sergeev, in his second career start (AP). MacKinnon one-timed the opener top shelf at 14:13 of the first. Blackwood then made a sprawling glove save on Matt Coronato with 8.5 seconds left in the period and stopped Joel Farabee 12 seconds into the second (AP). Colorado scored three in the first 10 minutes of the second. Necas finished Kadri’s feed on the power play at 2:37, with 18 seconds left on the man advantage. Lehkonen took Makar’s pass and wristed in his 11th at 5:48, and Nelson batted an airborne puck in on the backhand at 9:07 for 4–0 (NHL.com; Colorado Hockey Now). Farabee scored off a Kadri turnover at 10:50, and moments later Nelson took a Makar shot off the side of his helmet. He went down the tunnel but returned for the third (AP). Martin Pospisil made it 4–2 at 1:59 of the third, and Lehkonen deflected Makar’s point shot at 4:09 for his 12th, which ties him for second in the NHL (Colorado Hockey Now). Colorado wasn’t called for a penalty. “I thought Blackwood was exceptional, made big saves when we needed him,” Bednar said. He added that he wants to see the same game on the road (Colorado Hockey Now). With Manson out, Juulsen played on defense. Brindley, recalled Friday, dressed, while O’Connor (personal) was scratched (NHL boxscore).
