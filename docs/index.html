Last game

Wed Oct 14 vs Stars: L 3–2 (shootout). Colorado thought it scored twice in the first period, but neither goal counted. Jaden Schwartz’s goal was overturned when Dallas won an offside challenge. Gabriel Landeskog’s was waved off for a kicking motion (AP). Landeskog then got one that stood at 4:55 of the second, tipping Nazem Kadri’s shot from the left circle. Miro Heiskanen answered at 15:03 from Mikko Rantanen. Arttu Hyry stole a puck from Makar at the Dallas blue line and scored short-handed at 1:20 of the third. Brock Nelson tied it 2–2 at 8:21, burying a carom off Thomas Harley after Lehkonen’s shot. Gavin Brindley’s third-period shot hit both posts and stayed out. In the shootout, Jason Robertson and Hintz scored for Dallas, and Kadri scored for Colorado. Casey DeSmith stopped Necas, MacKinnon and Nelson for his 100th career win (NHL.com). Makar played 22:54 (+1, 2 shots) hours after twins Mattie and Rhett were born. The power play went 0-for-4.

nhl.com/avalanche news

Overnight / Thursday: No Avalanche game. The main news is the coaching change. Arizona State officially named Dave Hakstol head coach Thursday, and the Avalanche announced that former Avs forward Brett McLean is the new assistant on Jared Bednar’s staff. McLean was most recently an assistant with Vancouver (2025–26) and earlier with Minnesota / Iowa. Arena: nothing new on KSE’s ~$135M Ball Arena renovation (announced Sep 18).
