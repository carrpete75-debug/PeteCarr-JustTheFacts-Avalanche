Thursday, Nov 19, 2026 · Morning Edition · Avs fall 4–1 at Utah; Ducks visit tonight; MacKinnon and Makar still out

The Avalanche briefing

The Avalanche lost 4–1 at Utah on Wednesday night, ending the Mammoth’s six-game losing streak. Colorado trailed 3–0 after Utah’s short-handed goal 62 seconds into the third; Parker Kelly scored the lone Avs goal with 4:59 left, and Kevin Stenlund added an empty-netter. The Avs went 0-for-4 on the power play, and Mackenzie Blackwood took the loss. They are 12–7–3 with 27 pts, still 3rd in the Central. Tonight: Thu Nov 19 vs Anaheim, 7:00 PM MST at Ball Arena (KUSA / ALT / KTVD / Prime Video). DraftKings (via ESPN) has Colorado −130 / Anaheim +110 with a total of 6.5 (over −125 / under +105). Colorado is still without Nathan MacKinnon (out about a month) and Cale Makar (about two weeks), and Gavin Brindley is day to day. Kalshi has the Avs at 56¢. The Avs’ Cup price is unchanged at +800.

Published Thu Nov 19, 2026 · ~7:30 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Wednesday’s (Nov 18) edition.

Record

12–7–327 pts · 3rd in Central

Twenty-two games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 86 goals for, 68 against (+18). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 5–6–1 on the road, 6–2–2 in the last 10, and have lost one straight (NHL standings API). Minnesota leads the Central with 36 points in 23 games, and Dallas (13–6–2) has 28 in 21. Colorado is 3rd with 27 in 22 games, level on points with Nashville (27 in 22 GP, 11–6–5). In goal: Blackwood 11–4–1, .904, 2.56, 1 shutout (17 GP); Wedgewood 1–3–2, .863, 3.99 (6 GP). Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game (Wed Nov 18)

Wed Nov 18 at Mammoth: L 1–4. It was scoreless after one period. Liam O’Brien opened the scoring at 5:22 of the second with a one-timer from the faceoff circle (Yamamoto, Stenlund), his first goal of the season, and Vincent Trocheck made it 2–0 on the power play at 17:53 (Keller, Cooley). Dmitri Simashev scored short-handed 62 seconds into the third (Trocheck) for 3–0. Parker Kelly got Colorado on the board at 15:01 of the third (Schwartz), and Kevin Stenlund sealed it into an empty net at 17:52 (Hayton, Weegar). Sebastian Cossa stopped 30 of 31 shots for Utah; Blackwood stopped 25 of 28 (NHL box score). The Avs outshot Utah 31–29 but were 0-for-4 on the power play, including full two-minute chances to open the second and third periods; the third-period one ended with Simashev’s short-handed goal (AP; Colorado Hockey Now). Colorado’s five-game points streak (3-0-2) ended (NHL.com). Jared Bednar said he liked the first period, but “it kind of went away” and “too many guys” did not play well (NHL.com). Utah ended a six-game losing streak and is 11–9–3 (AP; NHL). MacKinnon and Makar sat out, and new defenseman Mike Reilly was a healthy scratch (NHL game sheet). Source disagreement: AP’s recap credits the Avs goal to Jaden Schwartz as unassisted; the NHL scoring summary, NHL.com’s recap and ColoradoAvalanche.com credit Parker Kelly with the goal (a redirect of Schwartz’s shot), which is the version shown here.
