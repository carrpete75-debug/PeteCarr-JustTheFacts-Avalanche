Sunday, Nov 8, 2026 · Morning Edition · Off day

The Avalanche briefing

Shutout at home. Colorado beat Detroit 4–0 Saturday. Martin Necas had two goals and two assists, Mackenzie Blackwood stopped all 27 shots and the power play went 3-for-5. The Avs are 10–6–2 (22 pts) and up to 3rd in the Central. It was Jared Bednar’s 800th game behind the Colorado bench. Next: a three-game Canadian trip that opens Tue Nov 10 at Toronto, 5:00 PM MST on ALT. DraftKings hasn’t posted that line yet. Kalshi has the Avs at 62¢, down from 71¢. DraftKings’ Central price shortened to +210.

Published Sun Nov 8, 2026 · ~7:40 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Saturday’s (Nov 7) edition.

Record

10–6–222 pts · 3rd in Central

Eighteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 72 goals for, 56 against (+16). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 3–5–0 on the road, 5–4–1 in the last 10, and they’ve won one straight (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central with 28 points in 19 games. Dallas and Colorado have 22 each; the Stars are 2nd because they’ve played one fewer game. Nashville and Utah have 21, St. Louis 16, Winnipeg 15 and Chicago 9. The Avs are 5th in the West and 11th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A); Necas 23 (9 G, 14 A); Makar 21 (5 G, 16 A in 17 games); Lehkonen 17 (team-high 12 G); Toews 16 (5 G, 11 A); Kadri 14 (5 G, 9 A); Schwartz 12 (7 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 9–3–1, .901, 2.60, 1 shutout; Wedgewood 1–3–1, .867, 4.02. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Sat Nov 7 vs Red Wings: W 4–0. Detroit had 13 of the first 14 shots, but Blackwood held the first period scoreless. His best stops came on J.T. Compher and John Leonard at 16:36–16:37 (ColoradoAvalanche.com). After the Red Wings killed two penalties, Necas finished a goal-mouth scramble on the power play at 9:46 of the second (T.J. Hughes, Makar). MacKinnon one-timed his 10th of the season, also on the power play, at 12:30 (Makar, Necas). Necas knocked in a rebound at 7:15 of the third (MacKinnon, Toews). Makar added Colorado’s third power-play goal at 9:45 (MacKinnon, Necas). Blackwood’s 27-save shutout was his first of the season and 18th of his career. Daniil Tarasov stopped 25 of 29 for Detroit, which has lost three straight. Gabriel Landeskog and Moritz Seider fought at 9:34 of the third. Milestones: it was Bednar’s 800th game as Avs coach, and his 455 wins are 5th-most through 800 games (AP). MacKinnon played his 968th game, passing Adam Foote for 3rd in franchise history. Makar’s 49th three-point game passed Milan Hejduk for 8th. Blackwood’s 54 wins in 90 Avs games tie Patrick Roy for 2nd-most through that span (NHL.com). Bednar: “There was no passengers on the bench tonight.”
