Friday, Nov 20, 2026 · Morning Edition · Avs blank Ducks 4–0; Makar placed on IR; no game until Wednesday

The Avalanche briefing

The Avalanche beat Anaheim 4–0 at Ball Arena on Thursday night. Brock Nelson scored twice and added an assist, Scott Wedgewood made 25 saves for his first shutout of the season, and the win ended Cutter Gauthier’s 10-game goal streak. They are 13–7–3 with 29 pts, still 3rd in the Central. Next: Wed Nov 25 vs Montréal, 7:30 PM MST at Ball Arena (ALT / KTVD). DraftKings has not posted a line for that game yet. Cale Makar was placed on injured reserve Thursday (he is out about two weeks from Nov 14) and Nathan MacKinnon is still out about a month; Gavin Brindley is out indefinitely, in concussion protocol per ESPN. Kalshi has the Avs at 60¢ to beat Montréal. The Avs’ Cup price is unchanged at +800.

Published Fri Nov 20, 2026 · ~7:30 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Thursday’s (Nov 19) edition.

Record

13–7–329 pts · 3rd in Central

Twenty-three games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 90 goals for, 68 against (+22). The Avs are 8–1–2 at home and 5–6–1 on the road, 6–2–2 in the last 10, and have won one straight (NHL standings API). Minnesota leads the Central with 38 points in 24 games, and Dallas (14–6–2) has 30 in 22. Colorado is 3rd with 29 in 23 games, two points ahead of Nashville (27 in 22 GP, 11–6–5). Utah and Winnipeg are at 25, St. Louis 24 and Chicago 14. The Avs are 5th in the West and 11th in the league (NHL standings API). Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A in 20 games; out about a month); Necas 26 (10 G, 16 A); Makar 23 (5 G, 18 A in 19 games; on IR); Lehkonen 21 (team-high 15 G); Toews 21 (7 G, 14 A); Kadri 18 (5 G, 13 A); Nelson 16 (8 G, 8 A); Schwartz 14 (7 G, 7 A); Landeskog 14 (8 G, 6 A). In goal: Blackwood 11–4–1, .904, 2.56, 1 shutout (17 GP); Wedgewood 2–3–2, .882, 3.38, 1 shutout (7 GP). Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game (Thu Nov 19)

Thu Nov 19 vs Ducks: W 4–0. It was scoreless after one period, with Wedgewood stopping all four shots he faced. Nicolas Roy opened the scoring eight seconds into the second period (Kadri, Landeskog), his fourth goal of the season. Brock Nelson made it 2–0 on the power play at 3:25 (Hughes, Toews) and 3–0 at 10:04 (Necas), his eighth goal of the year. Devon Toews closed the scoring at 10:33 of the third (Nelson, Necas). Wedgewood stopped all 25 shots for his 13th career shutout (first this season); Anaheim’s Lukas Dostal stopped 23 of 27 (NHL box score). The Avs went 1-for-3 on the power play and killed all three Ducks power plays (NHL; ColoradoAvalanche.com). Gauthier, who had scored in 10 straight games, went without a point for the first time since Oct. 22 (AP). Bednar credited the Lehkonen–Nelson–Necas line with shutting Gauthier down (ColoradoAvalanche.com). Defenseman Mike Reilly (claimed off waivers Nov 15) made his Avalanche debut and forward Adam Beckman, recalled that day, played his first NHL game since April 2024 (Puckbeat; Mile High Hockey). MacKinnon was scratched (injured) and Makar was out; Brindley and Gagné were scratched (NHL game sheet). Colorado is 13–7–3.
