Saturday, Oct 24, 2026 · Morning Edition

The Avalanche briefing

Avs 6, Lightning 3. Nathan MacKinnon had a goal and three assists and Martin Necas a goal and two as Colorado snapped a two-game skid at Ball Arena. Mackenzie Blackwood made 31 saves. The Avs are 6–4–1 (13 pts), 4th in the Central, level with Dallas. Next: Sunday at Nashville, 6:00 PM MDT on ALT / SCRIPPS; the Predators play at Pittsburgh tonight. Roster: Taylor Makar recalled, Logan O’Connor activated off LTIR (debut: 3 shots in 14:47), Zachary L’Heureux moved to LTIR. Sunday’s DraftKings lines are not posted yet. DK Cup +700, West +350, Central +170 (all flat). Kalshi has the Avs at 67¢ for Sunday.

Published Sat Oct 24, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:50 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Friday’s (Oct 23) edition.

Record

6–4–113 pts · 4th in Central

Eleven games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 47 goals for, 34 against (+13); 4–1–1 at home and 2–3–0 on the road; won one straight after back-to-back road losses (NHL.com). Colorado is level with Dallas on 13 points, but the Stars have played one fewer game and sit 3rd. Utah and Minnesota lead on 14, with the same 11 games played as the Avs. St. Louis and Winnipeg are two back on 11. Colorado is 7th in the West and 13th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 20 points (7 G, 13 A); Necas 14 (6 G, 8 A); Lehkonen 13 (team-high 9 G); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 10 games); Toews 9; Kadri 8; Nelson 8. In goal: Blackwood 5–3–0, .893, 2.91; Wedgewood 1–1–1, .912, 2.65. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Fri Oct 23 vs Lightning: W 6–3. Devon Toews scored 68 seconds in, from Necas and MacKinnon. Nazem Kadri then got his first goal of the season on a power play at 5:52, with Hagel in the box for tripping. Fedor Svechkov made it 3–0 just 24 seconds into the second. Jake Guentzel answered on a Tampa Bay power play at 8:13, and Artturi Lehkonen restored the three-goal lead at 13:57 off MacKinnon’s feed. That goal stood as the game-winner. Guentzel scored again with 42.7 seconds left in the second. Colorado unsuccessfully challenged that goal for a missed stoppage and took a delay-of-game penalty, but Tampa Bay couldn’t score on it (AP). MacKinnon made it 5–2 at 4:18 of the third with a wrist shot short side from inside the left dot, after Lehkonen sprang him (NHL.com). Charle-Edouard D’Astous cut it to 5–3 at 15:13, and Necas hit the empty net at 17:19. Tampa Bay outshot Colorado 34–24. “Beat one of the best teams in the League, so that’s always good,” MacKinnon said (NHL.com). It was his 45th career three-assist game, tying Joe Sakic for third in Avalanche/Nordiques history. Lehkonen extended his point streak to six games (6 G, 3 A). Logan O’Connor had three shots in 14:47 in his season debut (NHL.com).
