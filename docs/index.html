Wednesday, Nov 18, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day: at Utah tonight; MacKinnon and Makar still out; Wedgewood day to day (illness)

The Avalanche briefing

The Avalanche play at Utah tonight, still without Nathan MacKinnon (out about a month) and Cale Makar (about two weeks). Backup goalie Scott Wedgewood left Tuesday’s practice with an illness; Jared Bednar said he is “a little under the weather” and expects him ready for the back-to-back, and Mackenzie Blackwood is the projected starter (The Hockey News; Mile High Sticking). New defenseman Mike Reilly is expected to make his Avalanche debut. The Avs are 12–6–3 with 27 pts and have slipped to 3rd in the Central behind Minnesota and Dallas after others won Tuesday. Tonight: Wed Nov 18 at Utah, 8:00 PM MST (TNT / HBO MAX / ALT). DraftKings (via ESPN) has Utah −115 / Colorado −105 with a total of 6.5 (over −102 / under −118). Kalshi has the Avs at 50¢ (52¢ yesterday). Minnesota is the Central favorite at −155 (Avs +300), and the Avs’ Cup price lengthened to +800 from +750.

Published Wed Nov 18, 2026 · ~7:40 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Tuesday’s (Nov 17) edition.

Record

12–6–327 pts · 3rd in Central

Twenty-one games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 85 goals for, 64 against (+21). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 5–5–1 on the road, 6–2–2 in the last 10, and have won one straight (NHL standings API). They did not play Sunday, Monday or Tuesday. Minnesota leads the Central with 36 points in 23 games, and Dallas (13–6–2) has 28 in 21. Colorado is 3rd with 27 in 21 games, level on points with Nashville (27 in 22 GP, 11–6–5). Winnipeg and Utah have 23, St. Louis 22 and Chicago 14. The Avs are 5th in the West and 10th in the league (NHL standings API). Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A in 20 games; out about a month); Necas 24 (10 G, 14 A); Makar 23 (5 G, 18 A in 19 games; out about two weeks); Lehkonen 21 (team-high 15 G); Toews 19 (6 G, 13 A); Kadri 17 (5 G, 12 A); Nelson 13 (6 G, 7 A); Schwartz 13 (7 G, 6 A); Landeskog 13 (8 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 11–3–1, .905, 2.52, 1 shutout (16 GP); Wedgewood 1–3–2, .863, 3.99. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game (Sat Nov 14)

Sat Nov 14 at Canadiens: W 4–1. Colorado took the lead early: Fedor Svechkov scored on a snap shot at 3:57 of the first (O’Connor, Kulak), his 3rd, and Brock Nelson made it 2–0 at 15:55 (Lehkonen, Malinski), his 6th. Montreal got one back in the second when Nick Suzuki scored at 10:25 (Slafkovský, Caufield), a goal AP called a highlight-reel finish. In the third, Artturi Lehkonen restored the two-goal lead on the power play at 4:14 (Kadri, Hughes), his 15th, and Nicolas Roy added an empty-net goal at 19:47, unassisted. Mackenzie Blackwood stopped 25 of 26 shots; Jakub Dobes took the loss for Montreal, which had gone 12 straight games without a regulation loss (9–0–3) (AP). The Avs played without MacKinnon and Makar, who were hurt Thursday at Ottawa; defenseman Alex Gagne made his NHL debut and forward Gavin Brindley entered the lineup (NHL.com). Brent Burns played his 1,600th NHL game, the 14th player in NHL history to reach it and the only active one, and his consecutive-games streak stands at 1,028 (AP). Bednar on the loss of his two stars: “Now someone else has to be able to step up” (NHL.com).
