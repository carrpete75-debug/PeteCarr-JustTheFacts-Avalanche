Saturday, Oct 31, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day

The Avalanche briefing

Halloween at Ball Arena, minus Manson. The 7–5–1 Avs (15 pts, 3rd in the Central) host Calgary tonight at 8:00 PM MDT on ALT to close the homestand. Bednar said Friday that Josh Manson will miss about eight weeks with an ankle injury, so Noah Juulsen moves into the lineup. Colorado also recalled Gavin Brindley from the AHL, with Logan O’Connor day-to-day after his father’s funeral. The Flames (6–7–0) beat Seattle 6–2 Friday and arrive on a back-to-back. DraftKings has the Avs at −425, with a 6.5 total. Kalshi: 78¢ (+3¢).

Published Sat Oct 31, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:45 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Friday’s (Oct 30) edition.

Record

7–5–115 pts · 3rd in Central

Thirteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 54 goals for, 42 against (+12); 5–1–1 at home and 2–4–0 on the road; 5–4–1 in the last 10; one straight win (NHL.com). Idle Thursday and Friday. Minnesota leads the Central on 21 and Utah has 18. Colorado and Dallas have 15 each in 13 games, and the Avs rank 3rd on regulation wins (7 to Dallas’s 4). St. Louis and Nashville have 13, Winnipeg 11 (lost at Florida Friday) and Chicago 8. Colorado is 6th in the West and 16th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 22 points (8 G, 14 A); Necas 16 (6 G, 10 A); Lehkonen 15 (team-high 10 G); Toews 11 (4 G, 7 A); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 12 games); Kadri 10 (2 G, 8 A); Schwartz 9; Nelson 8; Landeskog 8. In goal: Blackwood 6–3–0, .894, 2.81; Wedgewood 1–2–1, .889, 3.48. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Wed Oct 28 vs Devils: W 5–2. New Jersey led on a short-handed goal. Connor Brown intercepted MacKinnon’s centering pass, broke out on a 2-on-1 with Luke Hughes, and his pass went in off Cale Makar’s skate and through Blackwood at 12:21 of the first (NHL.com). Josh Manson left at 15:19 of the first after falling awkwardly on a missed check; on Friday Bednar said he’ll miss about eight weeks with an ankle injury (NHL.com). Landeskog tied it on the power play at 10:58 of the second, finishing Schwartz’s cross-crease pass. The Avs outshot the Devils 21–6 in that period, but Jake Allen held. The third period broke it open. Kadri finished Nicolas Roy’s pass at 2:45, and Kelly beat Allen short side at 3:47. MacKinnon split Hughes and Brett Pesce for a breakaway goal at 12:00, and Schwartz scored on Malinski’s rebound 30 seconds later. Brown added a late backhand. “There was a simplicity to our offense. It was highly effective,” Bednar said. “I think it’s repeatable, regardless of your opponent” (NHL.com). Burns’ two assists gave him 950 career points, 10th among NHL defensemen. Roy had an assist in 11:26 in his return after six games out. Bednar’s 452nd win tied Joel Quenneville for ninth-most with one franchise, and Landeskog passed Cody McLeod for the franchise hits record (NHL.com).
