Sunday, Oct 25, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day

The Avalanche briefing

Game day: Avalanche at Nashville, 6:00 PM MDT on ALT / SCRIPPS. Colorado (6–4–1, 13 pts) was idle Saturday and is still 4th in the Central, but Utah and Minnesota both won and moved three points clear on 16. The Predators (3–3–4) lost 3–2 in overtime at Pittsburgh last night, so this is the second game of a back-to-back for them. DraftKings: COL −230 / NSH +190, puck line COL −1.5 (+110), total 6.5. Nicolas Roy is doubtful, and Colorado placed Georgii Merkulov on waivers Saturday. Starting goalies not confirmed. DK Cup +700, West +350, Central +170 (all flat). Kalshi has the Avs at 68¢ tonight (+1¢).

Published Sun Oct 25, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:25 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Saturday’s (Oct 24) edition.

Record

6–4–113 pts · 4th in Central

Eleven games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 47 goals for, 34 against (+13); 4–1–1 at home and 2–3–0 on the road; one straight win (NHL.com). Colorado didn’t play Saturday. Utah and Minnesota both won and lead the division on 16 points, three ahead of the Avs with one more game played (12). Dallas is level with Colorado on 13 but has played one fewer game and sits 3rd. Winnipeg and St. Louis are two back on 11. Colorado is 7th in the West and 14th in the league. Leaders (unchanged): MacKinnon 20 points (7 G, 13 A); Necas 14 (6 G, 8 A); Lehkonen 13 (team-high 9 G); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 10 games); Toews 9; Kadri 8; Nelson 8. In goal: Blackwood 5–3–0, .893, 2.91; Wedgewood 1–1–1, .912, 2.65. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game (no game Saturday)

Fri Oct 23 vs Lightning: W 6–3. Devon Toews scored 68 seconds in, from Necas and MacKinnon. Nazem Kadri then got his first goal of the season on a power play at 5:52, with Hagel in the box for tripping. Fedor Svechkov made it 3–0 just 24 seconds into the second. Jake Guentzel answered on a Tampa Bay power play at 8:13, and Artturi Lehkonen restored the three-goal lead at 13:57 off MacKinnon’s feed. That goal stood as the game-winner. Guentzel scored again with 42.7 seconds left in the second. Colorado unsuccessfully challenged that goal for a missed stoppage and took a delay-of-game penalty, but Tampa Bay couldn’t score on it (AP). MacKinnon made it 5–2 at 4:18 of the third with a wrist shot short side from inside the left dot, after Lehkonen sprang him (NHL.com). Charle-Edouard D’Astous cut it to 5–3 at 15:13, and Necas hit the empty net at 17:19. Tampa Bay outshot Colorado 34–24. “Beat one of the best teams in the League, so that’s always good,” MacKinnon said (NHL.com). It was his 45th career three-assist game, tying Joe Sakic for third in Avalanche/Nordiques history. Lehkonen extended his point streak to six games (6 G, 3 A). Logan O’Connor had three shots in 14:47 in his season debut (NHL.com).
