Record

3–1–06 pts · 2nd in Central

Four games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT). Colorado leads the NHL with 23 goals (5.8 per game, per the ESPN/AP preview). Winnipeg (3–0–1, 7 pts) leads the Central; Utah also has 6 points but has played five games. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the Central and first in the NHL. Since Wednesday’s edition

Wed Oct 7 @ Jets: L 2–3. Kyle Connor scored twice and Winnipeg won its third straight (AP headline via ESPN). It was Colorado’s first loss. Full box score details for this game weren’t re-pulled for this edition. No briefing was published Thursday.

Thu Oct 8 @ Flames: W 7–3. Makar had two goals and an assist, Toews and Schwartz each scored twice, and MacKinnon had a goal and two assists against the winless Flames (0–4–0). Scott Wedgewood made 26 saves in his season debut and 200th NHL game (AP). Zachary L’Heureux picked up an assist on MacKinnon’s goal. Logan O’Connor didn’t play following the sudden death of his father, Myles (ESPN).
