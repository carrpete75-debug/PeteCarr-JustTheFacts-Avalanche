Sunday, Nov 15, 2026 · Morning Edition · Avs beat Montreal 4–1 without MacKinnon and Makar

The Avalanche briefing

The Avalanche beat Montreal 4–1 on Saturday night without Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar, and coach Jared Bednar says MacKinnon is out about a month and Makar about two weeks. Both have lower-body injuries from Thursday’s overtime loss at Ottawa. Artturi Lehkonen had a goal and an assist, and Mackenzie Blackwood stopped 25 of 26. Colorado is 12–6–3 with 27 pts, 3rd in the Central (level with Nashville, which has played a game fewer). Next: Wed Nov 18 at Utah, 8:00 PM MST (TNT / HBO MAX / ALT). DraftKings has not posted a line yet. Kalshi has the Avs at 51¢ (49¢ yesterday). Minnesota is now the Central favorite at −125 (Avs +260).

Published Sun Nov 15, 2026 · ~7:25 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Saturday’s (Nov 14) edition.

Record

12–6–327 pts · 3rd in Central

Twenty-one games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 85 goals for, 64 against (+21). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 5–5–1 on the road, 6–2–2 in the last 10, and have won one straight (NHL standings API). Minnesota leads the Central with 34 points in 22 games. Nashville is 2nd with 27 in 20 games and Colorado is 3rd with 27 in 21. Dallas has 24 (19 GP), Utah and Winnipeg 23, St. Louis 20 and Chicago 12. The Avs are 5th in the West and 11th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A in 20 games; out about a month); Necas 24 (10 G, 14 A); Makar 23 (5 G, 18 A in 19 games; out about two weeks); Lehkonen 21 (team-high 15 G); Toews 19 (6 G, 13 A); Kadri 17 (5 G, 12 A); Nelson 13 (6 G, 7 A); Schwartz 13 (7 G, 6 A); Landeskog 13 (8 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 11–3–1, .905, 2.52, 1 shutout; Wedgewood 1–3–2, .863, 3.99. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game (Sat Nov 14)

Sat Nov 14 at Canadiens: W 4–1. Colorado took the lead early: Fedor Svechkov scored on a snap shot at 3:57 of the first (O’Connor, Kulak), his 3rd, and Brock Nelson made it 2–0 at 15:55 (Lehkonen, Malinski), his 6th. Montreal got one back in the second when Nick Suzuki scored at 10:25 (Slafkovský, Caufield), a goal AP called a highlight-reel finish. In the third, Artturi Lehkonen restored the two-goal lead on the power play at 4:14 (Kadri, Hughes), his 15th, and Nicolas Roy added an empty-net goal at 19:47, unassisted. Mackenzie Blackwood stopped 25 of 26 shots; Jakub Dobes took the loss for Montreal, which had gone 12 straight games without a regulation loss (9–0–3) (AP). The Avs played without MacKinnon and Makar, who were hurt Thursday at Ottawa; defenseman Alex Gagne made his NHL debut and forward Gavin Brindley entered the lineup (NHL.com). Brent Burns played his 1,600th NHL game, the 14th player in NHL history to reach it and the only active one, and his consecutive-games streak stands at 1,028 (AP). Bednar on the loss of his two stars: “Now someone else has to be able to step up” (NHL.com).
