Tuesday, Oct 6, 2026 · Morning Edition

The Avalanche briefing

2–0–0 (4 pts), now 2nd in Central after Winnipeg (2–0–1, 5 pts) won Monday at Pittsburgh. The Avs practice today, then open a three-games-in-four-nights stretch Wed @ Jets, 5:30 PM MDT (ALT). Bednar expects O’Connor and L’Heureux back on the trip, though neither has been activated from IR yet. DK Cup +750, West +320, Central +115 (all unchanged). Kalshi @ Jets 66¢, @ Flames 69¢, Central 48¢, Cup 13¢.

Published Tue Oct 6, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:50 AM MDT · Sources below each card

Record

2–0–04 pts · 2nd in Central

Two games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 14 goals for and 5 against. Colorado didn’t play Monday but slipped from 1st to 2nd in the NHL’s division order. Winnipeg won 3–2 at Pittsburgh to go 2–0–1 (5 pts) in three games. Minnesota (2–0–0) and Utah (2–1–0) also have 4 points. Dallas beat San Jose 5–0 for its first win (1–2–0). Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the Central and first in the NHL. Playoffs: reached the Western Conference Final and were swept 4–0 by Vegas (AP).

Last game · Sat Oct 3 vs Blues

Martin Necas scored twice and added an assist, Nathan MacKinnon had three assists, and the Avalanche routed the St. Louis Blues 6–1 at Ball Arena. Cale Makar opened the scoring at 4:09 of the first and Necas made it 2–0 later in the period. Then Josh Manson, Artturi Lehkonen, Nicolas Roy and Necas again blew it open with four goals in the second. Mackenzie Blackwood stopped 25 of 26 shots; Jordan Binnington allowed all six Colorado goals on 36 shots. Three stars: Necas, MacKinnon, Lehkonen.

Since then: The Avs have had three days off, with practices Sunday and today before they leave for Winnipeg. O’Connor and L’Heureux were full participants in regular jerseys Sunday. “I think we can see both those guys in Winnipeg and Calgary,” Bednar said (Denver Gazette). Captain Gabriel Landeskog missed Saturday’s morning skate and Sunday’s practice. Bednar called it “maintenance.” Landeskog’s head collided with Brandon Carlo in the third period Saturday, but he stayed in the game. The Gazette expects Scott Wedgewood to make his first start during the back-to-back.

Next: Wednesday at Winnipeg (5:30 PM MDT, ALT), Thursday at Calgary (7:00 PM MDT, ALT / KTVD), then Saturday vs Toronto (5:00 PM MDT, NHL Network / ALT).
