Wednesday, Nov 4, 2026 · Morning Edition · Off day

The Avalanche briefing

Blown out in Columbus. The Blue Jackets scored 28 seconds in, added four goals in 8:23 of the second period and beat the Avs 7–2. That snapped Colorado’s three-game winning streak. Makar and Kadri scored, and MacKinnon had two assists. Wedgewood was pulled after allowing four goals on nine shots. The Avs are 9–6–1 (19 pts) and fell from 2nd to 4th in the Central. “Now I have to change my approach,” Bednar said, including how he hands out ice time (Denver Gazette). Off today. Next: Thursday vs Nashville, 7:30 PM MST, streaming nationally on ESPN+ / Hulu / Disney+ (no ALT). DraftKings hasn’t posted lines yet. Kalshi has the Avs at 67¢. Kalshi’s Central market fell to 22¢ on its last trade (quote 23¢/32¢).

Published Wed Nov 4, 2026 · ~7:40 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Tuesday’s (Nov 3) edition.

Record

9–6–119 pts · 4th in Central

Sixteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 66 goals for, 53 against (+13). The Avs are 6–1–1 at home but 3–5–0 on the road, 5–4–1 in the last 10, and lost their last game (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central with 25 points in 17 games, and Utah has 20 in 16. Dallas and Colorado have 19 each, and the Stars have played one fewer game (15). Nashville has 17, St. Louis 15, Winnipeg 13 and Chicago 9. The Avs are 6th in the West and 11th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 27 points (9 G, 18 A); Necas 18 (7 G, 11 A); Makar 18 (4 G, 14 A in 15 games); Lehkonen 17 (team-high 12 G); Toews 15 (5 G, 10 A); Kadri 14 (5 G, 9 A). In goal: Blackwood 8–3–0, .894, 2.80; Wedgewood 1–3–1, .867, 4.02. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Tue Nov 3 at Blue Jackets: L 2–7. This was the second half of a back-to-back. Mathieu Olivier scored from the doorstep 28 seconds in (Coyle). Ivan Provorov made it 2–0 on a power-play one-timer at 7:37, after Colorado was called for too many men. Makar answered at 10:57 off MacKinnon’s cross-ice feed (Malinski also assisted). Then Columbus scored four times in 8:23 of the second period (NHL.com). Jake Christiansen scored from the point at 4:19, and Cole Sillinger scored on a rebound at 7:20. That chased Wedgewood after four goals on nine shots in 27:20. Charlie Coyle scored on the power play at 12:03, and Valeri Nichushkin made it 6–1 off the rush at 12:42. Kadri scored on a 5-on-3 power play at 16:35 (MacKinnon, Makar), his fifth goal in six games (Denver Gazette). Coyle scored again at 11:21 of the third and finished with two goals and two assists, matching his career high for points (NHL.com). Blackwood stopped 12 of 15 in relief. Cam Talbot made 27 saves for Columbus. Colorado outshot Columbus 29–24. The Avs have now lost five road games in regulation (Denver Gazette). Bednar: “It’s hot, cold, hot, cold right now, so it’s about consistency. Now I have to change my approach and the way I handle the inconsistencies in our game.” He added that this includes “distributing ice-time” and how he runs the bench (Denver Gazette).
