Last game

Sat Oct 10 vs Maple Leafs: L 3–6. MacKinnon tied it in the first and then gave Colorado a 2–1 lead at 15:14. After Nylander’s power-play goal, Brock Nelson made it 3–2 midway through the second. Toronto then took over: Nylander scored at 16:47 and Easton Cowan 46 seconds later. Matthews added a power-play goal in the third and an empty-netter. Mackenzie Blackwood made 17 saves on 22 shots (NHL) and took his first loss of the season. The Avs outshot Toronto 33–23 but went 0-for-5 on the power play, including eight seconds of 5-on-3. Nylander’s goal was the first power-play goal Colorado has allowed this season. It was the Avs’ first home loss in three games (AP). Logan O’Connor remains away from the team following his father’s death.
