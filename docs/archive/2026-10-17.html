Record

5–2–111 pts · 2nd in Central

Eight games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 36 goals for, 24 against; 3–1–1 at home and 2–1–0 on the road. Utah (6–2–0, 12 pts) leads the Central. Colorado and Minnesota both have 11 points in 8 games, and the NHL lists the Avs 2nd. Winnipeg (10 pts) has a game in hand. Leaders: MacKinnon 16 points (6 G, 10 A), Necas 10, Makar 9, Lehkonen 8 (team-high-tying 6 G). Bednar after the win: “we’re just having a tough time right now putting together a full 60” (NHL.com); Colorado has alternated wins and losses over its last seven. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Fri Oct 16 vs Penguins: W 5–2. Evgeni Malkin (8:28) and Bryan Rust (8:57, from Sidney Crosby) scored 29 seconds apart in the first period for a 2–0 Pittsburgh lead. Crosby’s assist was the 1,110th of his career, moving him past Joe Thornton into sole possession of seventh in NHL history. Lehkonen answered at 11:01 off a MacKinnon rush and tied it 25 seconds into the second, backhanding MacKinnon’s rebound five-hole. MacKinnon was awarded a penalty shot at 4:09 after Erik Karlsson hooked him on a breakaway, but Arturs Silovs stopped it with his left pad. Fifteen seconds later, Gabriel Landeskog swept in Brock Nelson’s pass for a 3–2 lead. It was his 83rd career go-ahead goal, tying Mikko Rantanen for sixth in Avalanche history (AP). Hughes tapped in a MacKinnon feed at 14:57 for his first NHL goal, and MacKinnon one-timed Makar’s pass on the power play at 15:06 of the third. Mackenzie Blackwood settled in after the shaky start and made 29 saves. It was the Avs’ first multigoal comeback win of the season, and their first at home since Apr 8, 2025. MacKinnon’s 44th career three-assist game tied Peter Forsberg for the most in Avalanche history, counting from 1995–96 (NHL.com). The power play went 1-for-4 in new assistant Brett McLean’s first game running it (AP).
