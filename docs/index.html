Tuesday, Nov 3, 2026 · Morning Edition · Game day

The Avalanche briefing

Three straight wins, and back at it tonight. Nazem Kadri and Jaden Schwartz scored twice each as the Avs came back from 0–2 to win 5–2 at Chicago. They’re now 9–5–1 (19 pts) and 2nd in the Central. Tonight is the second half of the back-to-back, at Columbus, 5:00 PM MST on ALT. Scott Wedgewood is confirmed to start (Daily Faceoff). DraftKings has Colorado at −130 (CBJ +110), total 6.5. Kalshi has the Avs at 53¢ (−3¢). D Keaton Middleton was recalled from the Eagles Monday and scratched. O’Connor returned to the lineup. DraftKings’ Cup price eased to +750.

Published Tue Nov 3, 2026 · ~7:40 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Monday’s (Nov 2) edition.

Record

9–5–119 pts · 2nd in Central

Fifteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 64 goals for (most in the NHL, per AP), 46 against (+18); 6–1–1 at home and 3–4–0 on the road; 6–3–1 in the last 10; three straight wins (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central with 23 points in 16 games. Colorado is 2nd with 19 in 15 games, ahead of Utah (18 in 15) and Dallas (17 in 14). St. Louis and Nashville have 15, Winnipeg 11 and Chicago 9. The Avs are 4th in the West and 7th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 25 points (9 G, 16 A); Necas 18 (7 G, 11 A); Lehkonen 17 (team-high 12 G); Makar 16 (3 G, 13 A in 14 games); Toews 15 (5 G, 10 A); Kadri 13 (4 G, 9 A). In goal: Blackwood 8–3–0, .899, 2.66; Wedgewood 1–2–1, .889, 3.48. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Mon Nov 2 at Blackhawks: W 5–2. Chicago went up 2–0 in the first period. Defenseman Wyatt Kaiser scored 57 seconds in (Donato assisting), and rookie Roman Kantserov finished a 2-on-1 from Connor Bedard at 7:05 for his sixth. That assist extended Bedard’s point streak to six games (AP). Colorado tied it with two goals 26 seconds apart. Kadri poked a loose puck over the goal line at 15:23 (Landeskog, Burns), and a wide-open Schwartz scored from the left circle at 15:49 (T.J. Hughes, Toews). The Avs outshot Chicago 14–4 in the period (NHL). Bedard was called for holding Kadri at 13:10 of the second, and on that power play Kadri tipped in Makar’s wrist shot at 13:38 for the game-winner (MacKinnon also assisted). Schwartz tipped in his sixth with 11 seconds left in the period (Malinski, Hughes). Toews hit the empty net with 2:22 left. Blackwood made 21 saves. All four of Kadri’s goals this season have come in the last six games, and Schwartz has three goals and an assist in his last three (AP). Colorado is 15–4–0 in its last 19 games against Chicago. Chicago’s Patrick Kane left in the second period with an upper-body injury (AP). O’Connor returned at fourth-line right wing (11:15). Manson (injured) and the newly recalled Middleton were scratched (NHL boxscore and right-rail).
