Record

4–2–19 pts · 3rd in Central

Seven games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 31 goals for, 22 against; 2–1–1 at home and 2–1–0 on the road. Utah (5–2–0, 10 pts) leads the Central. Winnipeg (9 pts in 6 GP), Colorado and Minnesota (9 each in 7) are next. Leaders: MacKinnon 12 points (5 G, 7 A); Necas and Makar 7 each. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Wed Oct 14 vs Stars: L 3–2 (shootout). Colorado thought it scored twice in the first period, but neither goal counted. Jaden Schwartz’s goal was overturned when Dallas won an offside challenge. Gabriel Landeskog’s was waved off for a kicking motion (AP). Landeskog then got one that stood at 4:55 of the second, tipping Nazem Kadri’s shot from the left circle. Miro Heiskanen answered at 15:03 from Mikko Rantanen. Arttu Hyry stole a puck from Makar at the Dallas blue line and scored short-handed at 1:20 of the third. Brock Nelson tied it 2–2 at 8:21, burying a carom off Thomas Harley after Lehkonen’s shot. Gavin Brindley’s third-period shot hit both posts and stayed out. Hyry also hit the post in overtime. In the shootout, Jason Robertson and Hintz scored for Dallas, and Kadri scored for Colorado. Casey DeSmith stopped Necas, MacKinnon and Nelson for his 100th career win (NHL.com). Makar played 22:54 (+1, 2 shots). Bednar: “Loved the first, loved the third. I didn’t love our habits and discipline … in the second period.” The power play went 0-for-4.
