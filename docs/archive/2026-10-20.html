Tuesday, Oct 20, 2026 · Morning Edition

The Avalanche briefing

Flat in Philadelphia; back at it tonight. The Flyers, who had one win entering the night, beat Colorado 3–2 Monday. Matvei Michkov scored twice and Trevor Zegras had three assists; Martin Necas and T.J. Hughes scored for the Avs. Bednar called it “sluggish and sloppy.” Colorado is 5–3–1 (11 pts) and 3rd in the Central. Tonight @ Devils at 5:00 PM MDT on ALT / KTVD, the second half of a back-to-back. Scott Wedgewood is expected in goal. DraftKings has it a pick’em: COL −110 / NJD −110, total 6.5. Logan O’Connor is set to debut Friday vs Tampa Bay. Roy is expected to start skating this week; L’Heureux is “a little further away.” No roster moves. DK Cup +700 (flat), West +350, Central Div +170 (from +130). Kalshi has tonight at 50¢.

Published Tue Oct 20, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:40 AM MDT · Sources below each card · No Oct 19 edition was published; changes below are measured against Sunday’s (Oct 18) edition.

Record

5–3–111 pts · 3rd in Central

Nine games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 38 goals for, 27 against (+11); 3–1–1 at home and 2–2–0 on the road. Monday’s loss ended a one-game win streak. Minnesota (6–2–1, 13 pts) beat Columbus 2–1 Sunday and Utah (7–3–0, 14 pts) beat Pittsburgh 5–4 Monday, so Minnesota passed the Avs, who drop from 2nd to 3rd. Colorado is 5th in the West and 11th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 16 points (6 G, 10 A), Necas 11 (5 G, 6 A), Lehkonen 9 (6 G), Makar 9 (in 8 games), Nelson 7, Toews 7. In goal: Blackwood 4–3–0, .889, 2.90; Wedgewood 1–0–1, .902, 2.40. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Mon Oct 19 @ Flyers: L 2–3. Philadelphia scored first again. With Colorado shorthanded for too many men (Fedor Svechkov jumped on early, per the Denver Post), Zegras’s slap-pass hit Denver Barkey in front and popped to Michkov for a power-play goal at 7:41 of the first. Bednar challenged for goaltender interference on Porter Martone; the officials ruled Josh Manson pushed Martone into Mackenzie Blackwood, so the goal stood and the Avs were charged a delay-of-game minor. “I thought it was goalie interference. They didn’t think so,” Bednar said (Denver Post). The Flyers outshot Colorado 13–8 in the first period. Michkov made it 2–0 at 12:23 of the second, circling the zone and firing through traffic. Necas answered at 17:52, three seconds after a power play ended, off a Lehkonen feed. Necas had briefly left in the first period after a Lehkonen shot came off the crossbar and hit him in the face (Denver Post). Noel Acciari tipped Oliver Bonk’s point shot at 6:34 of the third for a 3–1 lead. Hughes scored at 14:40 off a Sam Malinski shot that deflected off Svechkov; it was his second goal in as many games. Blackwood was pulled with 2:02 left, but Colorado got one shot on goal with the extra attacker; Travis Sanheim blocked three attempts (NHL.com). Joseph Woll made 24 saves for his first win as a Flyer, and Cole Knuble made his NHL debut (AP). “Sluggish and sloppy,” Bednar said. “It’s hard to go anywhere when you’re beating the puck up” (NHL.com).
