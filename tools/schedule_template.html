Sources & notes

Dates, opponents, venues, US TV and results all come from the NHL’s official schedule data for the Avalanche. ALT is Altitude Sports, the Avs’ local broadcast. “TBD” means no US TV is listed yet. After each game, the time is replaced by the result (W, L, or OTL/SOL for an overtime or shootout loss; Avs score first) and the running W–L–OT record. Preseason and regular-season records are counted separately.

Tickets appear for upcoming games only, and every link goes to an official seller. {{TIX_EVENT}} link to the specific game, taken from the host team’s link in the NHL schedule data (Avalanche home games via the Avs’ own avs.social link to Ticketmaster). {{TIX_GENERAL}} go to the host team’s official tickets page because no game link is published. No resale or affiliate links.
