Thursday, Oct 29, 2026 · Morning Edition · Off day

The Avalanche briefing

Avs 5, Devils 2. Colorado scored four times in the third period, with Kadri and Kelly 1:02 apart early on and then MacKinnon and Schwartz 30 seconds apart, to end New Jersey’s four-game winning streak. Blackwood made 20 saves in his 300th NHL game, and the Avs outshot the Devils 44–22. Colorado is 7–5–1 (15 pts), 4th in the Central and level with Dallas. The cost: Josh Manson (lower body) left in the first period, and Jared Bednar said he’s “going to miss some time.” Next: Sat vs Calgary, 8:00 PM MDT, ALT. DraftKings lines aren’t posted yet. Kalshi has the Avs at 77¢ Saturday.

Published Thu Oct 29, 2026 · Morning Edition · ~7:35 AM MDT · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Wednesday’s (Oct 28) edition.

Record

7–5–115 pts · 4th in Central

Thirteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 54 goals for, 42 against (+12); 5–1–1 at home and 2–4–0 on the road; 5–4–1 in the last 10; one straight win (NHL.com). Minnesota leads the Central on 20, Utah has 16, and Dallas and Colorado have 15 each. Dallas ranks 3rd with a game in hand. Nashville is 13, and St. Louis and Winnipeg are 11. Colorado is 7th in the West and 15th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 22 points (8 G, 14 A); Necas 16 (6 G, 10 A); Lehkonen 15 (team-high 10 G); Toews 11 (4 G, 7 A); Makar 11 (3 G, 8 A in 12 games); Kadri 10 (2 G, 8 A); Schwartz 9; Nelson 8; Landeskog 8. In goal: Blackwood 6–3–0, .894, 2.81; Wedgewood 1–2–1, .889, 3.48. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Wed Oct 28 vs Devils: W 5–2. New Jersey led on a short-handed goal. Connor Brown intercepted MacKinnon’s centering pass, broke out on a 2-on-1 with Luke Hughes, and his pass went in off Cale Makar’s skate and through Blackwood at 12:21 of the first (NHL.com). Josh Manson left at 15:19 of the first after falling awkwardly on a missed check. Landeskog tied it on the power play at 10:58 of the second, finishing Schwartz’s cross-crease pass. The Avs outshot the Devils 21–6 in that period, but Jake Allen held. The third period broke it open. Kadri finished Nicolas Roy’s pass at 2:45, and Kelly beat Allen short side at 3:47. MacKinnon split Hughes and Brett Pesce for a breakaway goal at 12:00, and Schwartz scored on Malinski’s rebound 30 seconds later. Brown added a late backhand. “There was a simplicity to our offense. It was highly effective,” Bednar said. “I think it’s repeatable, regardless of your opponent” (NHL.com). Burns’ two assists gave him 950 career points, 10th among NHL defensemen. Roy had an assist in 11:26 in his return after six games out. Bednar’s 452nd win tied Joel Quenneville for ninth-most with one franchise, and Landeskog passed Cody McLeod for the franchise hits record (NHL.com).
