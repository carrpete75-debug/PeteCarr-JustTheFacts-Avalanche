Record

2–0–04 pts · 1st in Central

Two games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT), 14 goals for and 5 against. The NHL’s division order is Colorado 1st, Minnesota 2nd (2–0–0) and Utah 3rd (2–1–0 after Sunday’s 4–2 loss at the Rangers). All three have 4 points. Winnipeg, Wednesday’s opponent, moved to 1–0–1 with a 3–2 win at Detroit Sunday. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the Central and first in the NHL. Playoffs: reached the Western Conference Final and were swept 4–0 by Vegas (AP).

Last game · Sat Oct 3 vs Blues

Martin Necas scored twice and added an assist, Nathan MacKinnon had three assists, and the Avalanche routed the St. Louis Blues 6–1 at Ball Arena. Cale Makar opened the scoring at 4:09 of the first and Necas made it 2–0 later in the period. Josh Manson, Artturi Lehkonen, Nicolas Roy and Necas again then blew it open with four goals in the second. Mackenzie Blackwood stopped 25 of 26 shots; Jordan Binnington allowed all six Colorado goals on 36 shots. Three stars: Necas, MacKinnon, Lehkonen.

Since then: The team practiced Sunday. Captain Gabriel Landeskog missed the session, which Bednar called “just maintenance.” Parker Kelly wore a non-contact sweater, likely as a precaution after taking a shot to the chest Saturday. Lehkonen has three goals in two games, the best two-game start of his career, in the final year of his contract (The Hockey News).

Next: A back-to-back road trip: Wednesday at Winnipeg (5:30 PM MDT, ALT), then Thursday at Calgary (7:00 PM MDT, ALT / KTVD).
