Wednesday, Nov 11, 2026 · Morning Edition · Off day

The Avalanche briefing

Avs 6, Maple Leafs 3. Colorado (11–6–2, 24 pts, now 2nd in the Central) won at Toronto on Tuesday behind two goals from Gabriel Landeskog, including the go-ahead goal 51 seconds after Toronto tied it 3–3 early in the third. Toews had a goal and an assist, Makar two assists, and Blackwood stopped 28 of 31. Cale Makar, who left Sunday’s practice early, played and had two assists. Next: Thursday at Ottawa, 5:00 PM MST on ALT. DraftKings has not posted a line yet; Kalshi has the Avs at 55¢. Cup odds are unchanged at +700.

Published Wed Nov 11, 2026 · ~7:45 AM MST · Sources below each card · Changes are measured against Tuesday’s (Nov 10) edition.

Record

11–6–224 pts · 2nd in Central

Nineteen games into 2026–27 (W–L–OT): 78 goals for, 59 against (+19). The Avs are 7–1–2 at home and 4–5–0 on the road, 6–3–1 in the last 10, and they’ve won two straight (NHL standings API). Minnesota leads the Central with 30 points in 20 games. Colorado is 2nd with 24, ahead of Nashville (23 in 18 GP), Dallas (22 in 17 GP) and Utah (21). Winnipeg has 19, St. Louis 18 and Chicago 9. Colorado has points in six of its last seven games (5–1–1, NHL.com). The Avs are 4th in the West and 9th in the league. Leaders: MacKinnon 30 points (10 G, 20 A); Necas 24 (10 G, 14 A); Makar 23 (5 G, 18 A in 18 games); Lehkonen 18 (team-high 13 G); Toews 18 (6 G, 12 A); Kadri 15 (5 G, 10 A); Schwartz 13 (7 G, 6 A); Landeskog 12 (7 G, 5 A). In goal: Blackwood 10–3–1, .901, 2.63, 1 shutout; Wedgewood 1–3–1, .867, 4.02. Last season (2025–26): 55–16–11, 121 points, first in the NHL. Playoffs: swept 4–0 by Vegas in the Western Conference Final (AP).

Last game

Tue Nov 10 at Maple Leafs: W 6–3. Darren Raddysh’s one-timer at 3:33 of the first (Matthews, Roslovic) gave Toronto the early lead, his first goal as a Leaf. Colorado answered late in the period: Brock Nelson tied it at 16:09 (Hughes, Schwartz) and Landeskog jammed in a rebound at 17:26 for a 2–1 lead, 77 seconds later (Roy, Makar). Toews made it 3–1 at 3:31 of the second with a point shot through traffic (O’Connor, Kelly). Easton Cowan cut it to 3–2 at 19:31 of the second (McKenna, Paul), and Jack Roslovic tied it 3–3 on a breakaway at 4:11 of the third (Cowan, Raddysh). Colorado answered 51 seconds later: Landeskog redirected Kadri’s cross-crease pass at 5:02 for his second of the game and the winner (Kadri, Malinski). Lehkonen made it 5–3 on a breakaway at 17:15 (Makar, Toews), and Necas scored into an empty net at 17:30 for the 6–3 final (his 10th). Blackwood stopped 28 of 31; Sergei Bobrovsky stopped 22 of 27 for Toronto, which has lost four in a row (AP). Thirteen different Avs recorded a point (ColoradoAvalanche.com). Makar had left Sunday’s practice early with a lower-body issue but declared himself ready after the morning skate and had two assists, five over the last two games (Denver Sports; NHL.com). Bednar: “I think it’s as close as you can get [to a 60-minute effort].”
